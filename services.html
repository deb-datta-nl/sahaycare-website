Our Services

Comprehensive medical and non-medical support for your loved ones, coordinated end-to-end by our care team.

🏥

Home Care

Sahay provides quality care with certified nurses, attendants, and physiotherapists from carefully chosen providers, overseen and coordinated by our team. We match the right caregiver to your loved one’s needs, whether that’s daily assistance, post-surgery recovery, or long-term companionship.

🚨

Emergency Response

Sahay works with local hospitals and emergency providers to give your loved ones speedy critical care exactly when it’s needed most. Our care managers coordinate directly with responders so no time is lost.

💊

Medicine Delivery

We work with local, authorised pharmacies to provide and deliver medicines on time to your loved ones — so prescriptions are never missed, even when family is far away.

🏥️

Hospitalization Support

Our care managers accompany your loved one on visits to medical clinics and help manage hospitalization should the need arise, including admission logistics and coordination with hospital staff.

🚗

Mobility

Sahay works with local mobility providers to arrange planned transport as needed, for appointments, errands, or social visits. Care managers can accompany if required.

📱

Non-medical Assistance

Support with non-medical requirements such as bill payments, banking services, and technology support — including help with smartphones, laptops, and staying connected with family.
